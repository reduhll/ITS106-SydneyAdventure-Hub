Before you go

  • Meeting point
    Each confirmation email lists an exact meeting spot; most trips depart from Circular Quay or a Blue Mountains trailhead car park. Arrive 15 minutes early.
  • What to bring
    Comfortable walking shoes, a refillable water bottle, sun protection, and a light layer for cooler mornings. Water-based trips supply life jackets and dry bags.
  • Age & accessibility
    Most walking trips suit ages 8+; the wildlife sanctuary and river cruise are family-friendly for all ages. Contact us before booking if step-free access is needed — several routes can be adapted.
  • Cancellations
    Free cancellation up to 48 hours before departure. Inside 48 hours, trips can be rescheduled once at no extra cost, subject to availability.
  • Safety
    All guides carry first-aid kits and satellite communication on remote trails. Trips can be postponed in the event of severe weather; we'll contact you directly if this affects your booking.
